比赛模式

两条赛道 · 评分体系

参赛队伍提交机器人策略代码，在 OmniGibson 仿真器中执行 50 项家庭任务， 以 Q-Score 为主排名指标，允许部分完成得分。

🔵 Standard Track · 标准赛道

  • 仅使用机载传感器观测（RGB + 深度）
  • 实例分割 + 本体感知数据
  • 不可访问模拟器内部状态
  • 最贴近真实机器人部署条件
  • MAGIKID 参赛赛道

🟡 Privileged Track · 特权赛道

  • 可访问模拟器任意内部信息
  • 包括物体精确位姿、场景点云等
  • 适合算法研究与上界探索
  • 独立排名，不与标准赛道竞争

📊 Q-Score 评分机制

Q-Score 通过计算已完成的子目标比例来评估策略表现，并选择最佳匹配的目标条款进行评分。 即使任务未完全完成，只要取得有意义的阶段进展，即可获得部分分数，比二元成功率更平滑可靠。

辅助指标（效率维度）：
· 模拟时间消耗
· 导航总距离
· 手部位移量
· 50 项任务平均任务成功率

MAGIKID Robotics Program

From Robot Hand to Super-Humanoid

A 10-month structured curriculum bridging mechanical design, motion control, and AI — guiding students from building their first robotic gripper all the way to programming a full humanoid robot.

LV 01
🤖
Weeks 1 – 8  ·  2 Months

Robot Hand

Understand degrees of freedom, joint mechanics, and how a robotic gripper grasps and manipulates objects.

  • Introduction to DOF & gripper anatomy
  • Servo motors & actuation basics
  • 3D-printed finger design
  • Tendon-driven vs rigid-link mechanisms
  • Force & pressure sensing
  • Pick-and-place programming
  • Grasp planning fundamentals
  • Capstone: autonomous grasping demo
🎯 Milestone: Build & program a 3-DOF robotic gripper
LV 02
🦾
Weeks 9 – 16  ·  2 Months

Robot Arm

Design and control a multi-joint robotic arm. Learn kinematics, trajectory planning, and real-time feedback control.

  • Forward & inverse kinematics
  • Jacobian matrix & workspace analysis
  • PID control for joint angles
  • ROS 2 introduction & URDF modeling
  • Trajectory planning & interpolation
  • Vision-guided arm control
  • Collision avoidance strategies
  • Capstone: arm picks objects via camera
🎯 Milestone: 6-DOF arm controlled by vision pipeline
LV 03
🧠
Weeks 17 – 24  ·  2 Months

Motion Simulation

Simulate full-body robot motion in physics engines. Train motion policies using reinforcement learning on MuJoCo & Isaac Sim.

  • Physics simulation principles (MuJoCo)
  • Robot model import & scene setup
  • Reinforcement learning basics (PPO/SAC)
  • Reward function design
  • Sim-to-real transfer concepts
  • Motion capture data & retargeting
  • Whole-body motion optimization
  • Capstone: train a walking gait policy
🎯 Milestone: Trained locomotion policy in simulation
LV 04
🚶
Weeks 25 – 32  ·  2 Months

Humanoid Robot

Integrate everything into a full humanoid platform. Program whole-body control, task execution, and human–robot interaction.

  • Humanoid hardware overview (Unitree, H1)
  • Whole-body control (WBC) framework
  • Balance & bipedal locomotion
  • Loco-manipulation: walk + grasp
  • SLAM & navigation on humanoids
  • Language-instruction interfaces (LLM→action)
  • Safety & emergency stop systems
  • Capstone: humanoid completes a household task
🎯 Milestone: Humanoid autonomously navigates & picks an object
LV 05
✨
Weeks 33 – 40  ·  2 Months

Super-Humanoid

Push the frontier. Design AI systems that let humanoid robots learn, adapt, and collaborate on tasks never seen before — the BEHAVIOR-1K challenge level.

  • Foundation models for robotics (VLA)
  • BEHAVIOR-1K task framework & benchmark
  • Multi-modal perception (vision + language + touch)
  • Online learning & few-shot adaptation
  • Multi-agent & human-robot collaboration
  • Ethics, safety, and responsible AI in robotics
  • Stanford BEHAVIOR challenge strategy & submission
  • Final Demo Day: real or simulated challenge task
🏆 Final Milestone: Submit to Stanford BEHAVIOR Challenge
